Nigerian bank rates

Banks do not publish a standard free rate feed, so enter each bank's rates from its website, branch or treasury desk. One line per currency: USD,buy,sell (naira per 1 unit). Buy is what the bank pays you for the currency; sell is what you pay the bank.

The app opens the page, reads the rate table and fills the box below for you to check before saving. Pages that only publish a PDF cannot be read automatically.
